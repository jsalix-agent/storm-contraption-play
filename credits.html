Photographs behind the storm

The scenery, kite parts, storm details, and scraps use cropped and tinted derivatives of these real photographs. Alpha.3's umbrella, press, and radio silhouettes are authored invention shapes with these photographic collage textures, not literal photographs of those inventions:

  • Fairmount Waterworks turbine gears — Jack E. Boucher, Historic American Engineering Record / Library of Congress. Public domain (U.S. federal archival photograph). View the source on Wikimedia Commons ↗
  • Radio Transmitter Model TBL-13, U.S. Navy / Westinghouse — Daderot, National Electronics Museum. CC0. View the source on Wikimedia Commons ↗
Images are processed into the game's local WebP assets; the game does not load photography from third-party hosts while you play.
